About Us

Two Decades of Purpose-
Built Education Technology

ISH Information Systems came with a clear conviction: Indian higher education deserves technology specifically built for it, rather than software adapted from generic enterprise solutions designed for other industries or regions.

Our Story

From Identifying a Gap to Powering Premier Institutions

Over 25 years ago, our founders identified a growing issue in India's higher education system. Institutions were dealing with complex operations, including hundreds of thousands of applications, thousands of students, and the need to comply with evolving national accreditation standards. Unfortunately, they relied on makeshift tools and manual processes that were not the best options for tackling these challenges.

ISH Information Systems emerged as the answer. Headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, we brought together education domain experts and technology professionals who collectively understood both the operational realities of Indian colleges and the scalability demands of modern software.

Today, our platform is trusted by Symbiosis International University, MICA Ahmedabad, Crescent University, Maharashtra National Law University, MIT University Shillong, and 50+ premier institutions across India — processing over 6,00,000 applications annually and managing examinations for up to 1,50,000 simultaneous test-takers.
