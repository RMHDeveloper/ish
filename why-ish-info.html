What Sets ISH Info Apart

India-First, Not India-Adapted

NAAC, NIRF, and IQAC aren't configuration options bolted onto a foreign data model — they're foundational architecture, mapped natively to Indian regulatory frameworks and accreditation cycles.

  • Pre-built NAAC criterion-wise data structures and DVV-ready exports
  • NIRF parameter tracking mapped directly to ranking methodology
  • IQAC documentation and AQAR workflows built in, not custom-coded

The Only Platform Covering All Streams

Engineering, Medicine, Law, Management, Design, Architecture, Arts, Sciences, Commerce, Pharmacy, and Agriculture — dedicated, stream-specific admission and academic workflows in one unified system, not a one-size-fits-all form builder.

Built by Education Domain Experts

Our product and implementation teams bring years of hands-on experience working alongside Indian colleges, IQAC coordinators, admission directors, registrars, and controllers of examination — not generalist software consultants.

Proven at Scale — Not Theoretical

6,00,000+ applications processed annually. 1,50,000+ simultaneous test-takers. Field-tested at Symbiosis International, MICA, Maharashtra National Law University, Crescent University, and MIT University Shillong.

Fast Deployment, Lower Risk

Typical deployment timeline of 4–8 weeks with pre-built configurations for common Indian institutional structures — multi-campus setups, deemed-university governance, and autonomous-college workflows — instead of a 6–12 month generic ERP rollout.

Compliance-Native Architecture, Backed by Real Support

Pricing designed for Indian institutional budget cycles, paired with dedicated post-deployment support — continuous monitoring, compliance updates as NAAC/NIRF frameworks evolve, and proactive enhancement guidance, not a ticket queue.
