EzAcademics

Manage Every Student. From Day One, Graduation, and Placement.

EzAcademics is ISH Info's integrated Student Lifecycle Management System — a single, unified platform that tracks, manages, and optimizes every stage of a student's academic journey at your institution, from admission through graduation and placement.

Explore EzAcademic — Request a Demo
System Overview

One System. Every Stage of the Student Journey.

From initial onboarding through course progression, examination, and graduation, EzAcademic provides institutions with complete visibility and control over their academic operations. When student, faculty, and course data are made systematic and accessible, it delivers measurable value to every stakeholder — students, faculty, administration, and industry.

EzAcademics — Core Modules

Student Onboarding & Registration

Smooth transition from admitted applicant to enrolled student with digital documentation, ID generation, and program allotment.

Course & Program Management

Define programs, courses, electives, credits, and interdisciplinary tracks aligned with NEP 2020 flexible curricula.

Timetable & Scheduling

Automated timetable generation with room and faculty allocation — conflict-free scheduling at scale.

Attendance Management

Real-time attendance tracking with automated alerts for students approaching attendance thresholds.

Academic Records & Transcripts

Centralized student academic records with secure transcript generation and verification capabilities.

Examination Management

Internal examination scheduling, hall ticket generation, marks entry, results processing, and grade report generation.

Student Performance Analytics

Track individual and cohort academic performance trends, early warning indicators, and progression analytics.

OBE — Outcome-Based Education Mappings

Full curriculum-to-outcome mapping aligned with NAAC criteria and NEP-ready graduate attribute requirements.

Student Portal

Self-service portal for timetables, attendance, results, fee status, and academic communications.

Alum Transition

Seamless transition from active student to alum record with placement and outcome data capture for NIRF reporting.

NEP 2020 Alignment

NEP-Ready Academic Management

The National Education Policy (NEP) has introduced significant changes to how Indian institutions must structure and manage academic operations — multiple entry/exit pathways, credit transfer frameworks, multidisciplinary programs, and Indian Knowledge System curriculum requirements.

EzAcademics design aligns with NEP academic architecture, supporting flexible credit structures, credit transfer, and the documentation requirements of a multi-pathway academic model.

Multiple Entry/Exit

Flexible pathways with credit-linked documentation.

Credit Transfer

Cross-program and cross-institution credit frameworks.

NAAC & NIRF Impact

EzAcademic directly contributes to NAAC Criterion III (Research, Innovations) and Criterion II (Teaching-Learning & Evaluation) data collection. Accurate, system-generated academic records also improve NIRF's Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR) and Graduation Outcomes (GO) parameter scores.
