ISH Info's Online Fee Management System eliminates the reconciliation headaches, payment disputes, and compliance gaps that haunt institutional finance teams. Secure, automated, and built for the complexity of Indian higher education fee structures.

Fee Management Covers

100+ Payment Options

UPI, NEFT/RTGS, net banking, debit/credit cards, institutional challan — every payment mode Indian students expect.

Automated Reconciliation

Real-time payment reconciliation with bank feeds — zero manual matching, zero disputes on payment status.

Fee Structure Configuration

Tuition, hostel, transport, library, lab, examination fees — configure multi-component fee structures per program and batch.

Promotions, Waivers, Transfers & Cancellations

Manage scholarship adjustments, fee waivers, refund requests, and partial cancellations within the system.

Class/Subject Allotment Integration

Fee collections linked directly to course enrolment and subject allotment workflows.

Financial Reporting & Audit Trail

Complete audit-ready financial reports, collection summaries, and reconciliation statements for finance teams.

Defaulter Management

Automated fee defaulter alerts, communication workflows, and hold mechanisms for academic record access.

Multi-Campus Fee Management

Consolidate fee collection across multiple campuses, departments, and programs with campus-level breakdowns.

Student & Parent Portal

Self-service fee payment, receipt download, and fee statement access for students and parents.

Books of Accounts Integration

Seamless two-way integration with any books of accounts for complete institutional accounting and financial management.
