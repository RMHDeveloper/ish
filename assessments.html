Assessment Platform — Key Features

Scalable Architecture

Handles up to 1,50,000 simultaneous test-takers — proven for All India entrance and recruitment exams.

Proctored & Non-Proctored Modes

Full online proctoring (video, audio, screen monitoring) or standard non-proctored assessments as required.

Question Bank Management

Build, organize, and reuse question banks across subjects, topics, and difficulty levels with tagging and metadata.

Multiple Question Types

MCQs, descriptive, numerical, matching, and case-based question formats supported.

Secure Exam Delivery

Browser lockdown, IP restriction, OTP-based authentication, and randomized question/option ordering.

Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard

Live candidate status, section-wise progress, and exam completion metrics for administrators.

Automated Results Processing

Instant scoring for objective exams with configurable negative marking, partial marking, and sectional cut-offs.

Hall Ticket & Exam Centre Rostering

Automated generation of hall tickets, exam centre allocation, and invigilator assignment rosters.

Integrated with Admissions

Connect test performance data to admission decisions, merit list generation, and onboarding workflows.

Internal Exam Management

Manage internal assessments for 30,000+ students with faculty-configurable assignments, submissions, and grading.
