Convert Every Enquiry into an Enrolled Student

EzCRM — Lead Management Capabilities

Omni-Channel Lead Capture

Capture leads from paid ads, organic search, social media, web forms, WhatsApp, SMS, email, and offline events into a single dashboard.

Automated Lead Assignment

Round-robin distribution to admissions counsellors eliminates follow-up delays and ensures no lead goes uncontacted.

Lead Nurturing Campaigns

Automated email, SMS, and WhatsApp sequences that move leads from awareness to decision — personalized by program and stage.

Admissions Funnel Analytics

Real-time visibility into enquiry-to-application-to-enrolment conversion, counsellor performance, and source ROI.

UTM Campaign Attribution

Measure every rupee spent on admissions marketing — identify which channels drive the highest-quality applications.

Application Conversion Tracking

Track each enquiry's progression from lead to application, from application to admission, from admission to enrolment.

Counsellor Performance Dashboard

Supervisor visibility into individual counsellor productivity, response times, and conversion rates.

Student 360° Profile

Complete view of a prospective student's enquiry history, communications, documents, and application status in one record.

Integration with EzAdmissions

Seamless handoff from lead to application workflow — no data re-entry, no gaps in the applicant journey.

Custom Report Builder

Generate campaign performance, territory-wise, program-wise, and source-wise reports for institutional leadership.

Never Let a Lead Go Cold Again

Give your admissions team one dashboard for every enquiry, from first touch to enrolment.
